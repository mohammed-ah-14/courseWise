How a prediction is made

History in, a clear estimate out.

  1. 01

    Look back

    Grade distributions for every course, instructor and term since 2005, cleaned and merged by section.

  2. 02

    Learn the pattern

    A model learns how course history, instructor, term and class size shape results, then is tested on terms it never saw.

  3. 03

    Plan with it

    Typical error is about ±0.15 GPA points per course. Good enough to compare options and balance a term.
